About Me

I'm an AI Product Leader with 16 years of telecom experience, including 10 years in product management, building AI-powered B2B products — spanning conversational AI platforms, generative and agentic AI systems, real-time speech intelligence, and developer-facing API ecosystems. I commercialised Vonage's internal conversational AI platform into an external product line, opening it to the global developer ecosystem through new SI partnerships and enablement programs.

I run structured A/B testing of agentic NLU workflows against traditional NLU/ASR pipelines to guide model and product strategy, and I led the formation of Vonage's cross-departmental AI Governance Council under C-level sponsorship, cutting AI compliance review cycles by ~90%. Earlier, I was the first PM at a WebRTC analytics startup later acquired by 8x8, repositioning it from a developer debugging tool into an enterprise voice-quality intelligence platform.

I'm a co-inventor on granted US and European patents, a research contributor to the HTTP/2 and QUIC standards through IETF and W3C working groups, and an international conference speaker. My work has been recognized by IETF and W3C, and I believe in building products that leverage cutting-edge AI technology while delivering genuine business value through user-centric design and strategic implementation.
